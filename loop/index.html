Loop

Keep your word with your friends.

Loop is a small private app for a group of close friends: chat, plans, streaks and shared money, in one place.

Help Privacy Terms

Private by design

  • Invite only. No public profiles: someone outside your circles finds only your name and username, and only by searching for it.
  • No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.
  • Download everything or delete your account at any time.

Loop is in a private beta. If a friend invited you, open their invite link to get the app.
