Loop

Privacy policy

What Loop stores about you, who can see it, and how to remove it.

Help Privacy Terms

Last updated 20 October 2026

The short version

  • Loop is a private app for a small group of close friends. What you share goes to the people in that circle or chat, and to nobody else.
  • Loop has no ads, no advertising or tracking software, and never sells or rents your data.
  • Chats are encrypted on their way to our servers and while stored there, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. We look at content only for the reasons listed below.
  • You can download everything or delete your account at any time in the app: Settings, then Privacy and safety.

Who we are

Loop is made and run by RAFTZUM LLC ("we", "us"). We decide what happens to the data described here. To reach us, use the form on the help page or "Report a problem" in the app's Settings.

What Loop stores, and why

Your account

  • Your email address, used only to send you sign-in codes and to answer you if you write to us. Other people in Loop cannot see it.
  • Your profile: username, display name, profile photo, your birthday if you add it, and anything you add about yourself. Your friends and the people in your circles can see these.
  • Your time zone, so that streak days, reminders and plans line up with your day.

What you share

  • Messages, photos, voice notes, stickers, links and reactions; plans, polls and replies; streaks, check-ins and photo proof; shared expenses, bills and who owes whom; lists, albums, wishlists, games, moods, questions and answers; messages you schedule and reminders you set. The people in the circle or one-on-one chat where you share them can see them.
  • Notes about a friend that you keep for yourself. Only you can see them.
  • "Free tonight" and free hours. If you say you are free, or fill your free hours for a plan, the people you share it with see when you are free. When Loop fills free hours from your phone's calendar, it works out the busy times on your phone; event names and details never leave it.
  • Money in Loop is a record only. Loop never handles payments, bank details or cards.

Health totals, only if you turn on Health data in Settings

Daily totals for steps, exercise, workouts, sleep, mindful minutes and walking or running distance, read from Apple Health or Health Connect, so those streaks can update themselves. Only the day's total is sent, not the readings behind it. The friends on a streak see whether you reached its goal, and your number only where the streak shows it.

Notifications

To send you notifications, Loop stores a device token from Apple or Google. The text of a notification passes through Apple's Push Notification service or Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging on its way to your phone.

Keeping Loop working

  • Crash reports and usage, unless you turn them off (Settings, Privacy and safety, "Share crash reports and usage"): the error text, the screen you were on, the app version, your phone's model and system version, and how often each feature is used. Email addresses, long numbers and quoted text are removed before anything is sent. Never what you write, your photos or names.
  • "Report a problem": what you write, with the app version, phone model and the last few screens you opened.
  • Reports and blocks: when you report something, we receive the report and can see the content it is about. Blocks are stored so they keep working.
  • Messages from the help page: your email address, the topic and what you write.

What stays on your phone

  • Your calendar. Loop reads busy times to mark free hours. Event names and details never leave your phone.
  • Camera, microphone and photo library. Used only when you take, record or pick something. Nothing is uploaded until you send it. Scanning an invite's QR code does not upload anything.
  • Health readings. Only the daily totals above leave your phone.
  • Your sign-in session, unsent messages, read markers, pinned chats and display settings.

Loop does not use your location and does not read your contacts.

Health data

Health data is used only to update your streaks in Loop. It is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never given to data brokers or other third parties, and never stored in iCloud. Loop does not write to Apple Health or Health Connect. You can turn the switch off at any time in Settings, and remove Loop's access in the Health app or in Health Connect. Loop's use of data from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who can see your data

  • The people you share with: the members of a circle, the friend in a one-on-one chat, the people on a streak or plan.
  • Us, only when needed. Because chats are not end-to-end encrypted, the people who run Loop can technically access them. We do so only to review something that was reported to us, to fix a problem you reported, to keep Loop secure, or when the law requires it.
  • The companies that run parts of Loop for us, which may use the data only to provide their service to us:
    • Supabase: the database, file storage and sign-in. Servers in Canada.
    • Google: sending sign-in emails (Gmail) and delivering notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
    • Apple: delivering notifications to iPhones (Apple Push Notification service).
    • GitHub: hosting these web pages and keeping our encrypted nightly backups.
  • Authorities, when a valid legal request requires it. United States law also requires us to report apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and what you share: as long as your account exists, or until you delete the item.
  • When you delete your account: your account, profile, messages, and streak and money records are removed from the live database straight away. Your photos, voice notes, check-in photos and profile photo are removed from file storage within 30 days (usually within a day). Photos you added to a circle's shared album, receipts, files on a plan and stickers stay with that circle until someone in it deletes them or the circle is deleted. Encrypted backups are deleted after 30 days.
  • Crash reports and usage counts: 180 days. Problem reports and messages from the help page: up to one year.
  • Content removed after a report: kept out of sight for up to one year where the law requires us to preserve it, then deleted.

Your choices and rights

  • Download your data: Settings, Privacy and safety, Download my data.
  • Delete your account: Settings, Privacy and safety, Delete my account. If you no longer have the app, ask us through the help page.
  • Change your profile at any time, and turn off Health data, crash reports and notifications in Settings.
  • Block or report anyone from their profile or from a message.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or limit how it is used. Ask us through the help page and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. We use your data to provide the service you asked for, to keep it safe, and to meet legal duties.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access rules in the database let each person read only what was shared with them. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

Age

Loop is for people 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we will delete it.

Where your data is processed

Your data is stored in Canada. Google, Apple and GitHub may process it in the United States or other countries. Where the law requires it, these transfers are covered by the providers' standard safeguards.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new date. If a change matters for how your data is used, we will also say so in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Write to us through the help page, or in the app: Settings, then "Report a problem".
